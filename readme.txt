== Description ==

Bit CRM is a advance Sales CRM for WordPress. It manages the full path from first enquiry to paid invoice: leads, contacts, companies, deals, a visual sales pipeline, invoicing and WooCommerce customer sync, all inside from one dashboard.

= How Bit CRM works =

A lead arrives from your website form or your store. You qualify it, then convert it into a contact and a company in one click. You open a deal, price it with line items, and move it across your pipeline stages. When the deal is won, you raise the invoice, apply taxes and discounts, and email the PDF. Every call, meeting, task and note stays attached to the record, so the full history is there when you need it.

= Why choose Bit CRM for WordPress? =

* **All-in-one CRM** – Contacts, leads, companies, deals and invoices work together in one plugin.
* **Self-hosted and private** – Your CRM records stay in your own WordPress database.
* **Built for sales** – A visual pipeline, deals and invoicing help you close and bill faster.
* **WooCommerce ready** – Connect your store customers, orders and products.
* **Easy to start** – Import your existing data from CSV and begin in minutes.

= Core CRM modules =

* **[Lead management](https://bit-crm.com/feature/lead-management/)** – Capture leads, track activity, add notes, then convert them into contacts, companies and deals, single or in bulk.
* **[Contact management](https://bit-crm.com/feature/contact-management/)** – Store and organize customer records with tags, filters, notes, attachments and related activities.

= Activities and follow-up =

Inside every lead, contact, company and deal you get a full set of engagement tools.

* **[Tasks](https://bit-crm.com/feature/task-management/)** – Set owners, due dates, priorities and status, then filter what is coming up.
* **[Meetings](https://bit-crm.com/feature/meeting-tracking/)** and **[calls](https://bit-crm.com/feature/call-tracking/)** – Log every interaction with owners, dates and status.
* **[Email, SMTP and IMAP](https://bit-crm.com/feature/send-receive-emails/)** – Send email from the CRM, configure SMTP for delivery, and connect a Gmail, Zoho or custom mailbox so conversations sync back into the record.

* WooCommerce products can be inserted as line items into any deal or invoice.

== Installation ==

= From your WordPress dashboard =

1. Go to Plugins > Add New.
2. Search for **Bit CRM**, or click Upload Plugin to upload the plugin zip file.
3. Click Install Now, then Activate.
4. Open the Bit CRM menu in your WordPress dashboard.

= Initial setup =

1. On first launch, Bit CRM opens a setup wizard. Enter your business name and email, or use Skip to add them later.
2. The next step offers Bit Form, Bit SMTP and Bit Integrations as optional companion plugins. They are selected by default, so deselect any plugin you do not want installed.
3. Click Let's get started to finish onboarding and open the dashboard.
4. Go to Settings > Currencies and review your home currency before creating any financial records.
5. Use Settings to configure business details, deal stages, entity fields, invoice preferences, email and integrations.
6. Open Leads, Contacts, Companies or Deals and use the add button to create your first record, or use Import to upload existing CSV data.

All core CRM features work inside WordPress. Optional integrations only need configuration when you choose to use them. Step-by-step guides are in the [Bit CRM documentation](https://bit-crm.com/docs/).

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is Bit CRM a complete CRM for WordPress? =

Yes. Bit CRM lets you manage contacts, leads, companies, deals and invoices from a single dashboard, so you can run your whole customer relationship workflow inside WordPress.

= Are there limits on how many contacts or invoices I can create? =

No. Bit CRM has no record or user caps. The only limits are the ones your own hosting applies, such as database size.

= What is included in this plugin, and what needs Bit CRM Pro? =

This plugin includes leads, contacts, companies, deals, invoicing, tasks, meetings, calls, notes, attachments, links, email with SMTP and IMAP, tags, advanced filters, CSV import, recycle bin, the dashboard, the external API and WooCommerce sync. Workflow automation, the client portal, the AI assistant, recurring invoices, the calendar, invoice payment links, the CRM product catalog, custom fields, team access, multi-currency, CSV export, activity history and the MCP server are in Bit CRM Pro.

= Where is my CRM data stored? =

In your own WordPress database, on your own hosting. Nothing is held in a third-party CRM service.

= Can I manage a sales pipeline with Bit CRM? =

Yes. Create deals and move them through pipeline stages you define, either as a sortable table or a drag-and-drop Kanban board that shows deal count and total value per stage.

= Can I create and send invoices? =

Yes. Build invoices with line items and terms, apply taxes and discounts, track status through draft, sent, paid and overdue, then download the PDF or email it. Online payment links are a Pro feature.

= Can I add products to deals and invoices? =

Yes. Add WooCommerce products as line items with their own quantity, discount and tax. A CRM-native product catalog is available in Bit CRM Pro.

= Can I use this as a WooCommerce CRM? =

Yes. Store customers and order history sync into contacts, contacts are auto-tagged by product and coupon, and WooCommerce products can be used as deal and invoice line items.

= Which other plugins does Bit CRM work with? =

WooCommerce, FluentCart, SureCart, LearnDash and Tutor LMS for customer sync, Bit Form for lead capture, Bit SMTP for email delivery, and Bit Integrations and Bit Flows for connecting to other apps.

= Can I connect my email to Bit CRM? =

Yes. Send emails to contacts from the CRM, set up SMTP for reliable delivery, and connect a Gmail, Zoho or custom IMAP mailbox to keep conversations in sync.

= Can I import my existing contacts? =

Yes. Use the built-in Import tool to bring contacts, leads, companies and deals in from a CSV file. Most other CRMs can export to CSV, so migrating is straightforward.

= Will I lose data if I delete a record by mistake? =

No. Deleted records go to the recycle bin, where you can restore them or permanently remove them when you choose.

= Will Bit CRM slow down my site? =

No. Bit CRM only runs in the WordPress admin, and heavy operations such as imports, bulk conversions, IMAP fetching and store syncing run as background jobs rather than during page loads.

= Does it work with my theme and page builder? =

Yes. Because Bit CRM works in the admin area, it does not affect how your site looks or loads.

= Is Bit CRM available in other languages? =

Yes. It ships with Arabic, French, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese translations, and the interface mirrors fully for right-to-left locales.

= Where can I get help? =

Read the [documentation](https://bit-crm.com/docs/), post in the WordPress.org support forum, or join the [community forum](https://forum.bit-crm.com/).

== External Services ==

Bit CRM stores your CRM records in your own WordPress database. The connections below are the only points where data leaves your site, and each one is described here.

= Google Fonts =

Bit CRM admin pages load the "Outfit" font from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), sending the administrator's IP address, user-agent and request data, but no CRM records. See Google's [Terms](https://policies.google.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

= Email and IMAP providers =

An administrator can connect [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/) (imap.gmail.com), [Zoho Mail](https://www.zoho.com/mail/) (imappro.zoho.com), or a custom IMAP server. On email history or sync, Bit CRM sends the saved mailbox username, app password, connection settings and IMAP commands to that provider, and retrieves matching message data. Gmail is governed by Google's [Terms](https://policies.google.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); Zoho by its [Terms](https://www.zoho.com/terms.html) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html); a custom server by its provider's policies.

When a user sends an email or invoice, Bit CRM passes its recipient, subject, body, headers and attachments to WordPress's `wp_mail`, which uses the site owner's configured mail service and the recipient's mail server. Those providers' terms and privacy policies apply. Bit CRM does not select a mail provider.
